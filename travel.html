Getting Here

By Flight

Mangaluru Int. Airport (IXE)

  • ~90 km south of Kundapura along the smooth NH-66 highway corridor.
  • Pre-paid airport taxis and cab rentals take ~1.5 to 2 hours.
  • Direct flights from BLR, BOM, DXB, DOH.
View on Map

By Train

Kundapura (KUDA) / Udupi (UD)

  • Kundapura Station is just 4 km away from the town center & venue.
  • Udupi Station is ~32 km away with frequent express trains (Panchaganga, Vande Bharat).
  • Auto rickshaws & cabs readily available at stations.
KUDA Map UD Map

By Road & Bus

NH-66 Highway Corridor

  • Enjoy superb four-lane connectivity along the coastal belt.
  • Direct luxury AC sleeper buses ply overnight daily from BLR, Mysuru, Mumbai, Pune, and Goa.
  • Drop off at Koteshwara Junction or Kundapura town.

November in Coastal Karnataka

Mid-November weather in Kundapura and Udupi is blissful and pleasant. The monsoon has passed, leaving lush greenery, clear skies, and gentle sea breezes.

What to Wear

Light ethnic silks, breathable linens, and comfortable festive attire are highly recommended for the coastal climate.
